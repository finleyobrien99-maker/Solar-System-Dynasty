# Earned epithets

Solar Dynasty has **57 lifetime epithets**. Player and AI rulers use the same requirements. Names come from actual deeds, family facts and time spent ruling, rather than starting traits, inherited land or wealth.

Every earned name stays in that person's history with its year and the deeds behind it. The most defining name appears beside their name; if two names are equally defining, the latest takes precedence. Heirs and clones start their own records. Named past rulers remain available in house profiles and the dynasty tree.

Profiles include the full catalogue and current progress. Existing saves remain compatible: past actions that were never recorded cannot be reconstructed, so deed counters begin when an older save is loaded with this update. Known family and age facts can still count.

The AI earns names for actions it actually performs: fleet building, income, wars, victories, conquests, plots, trade partnerships, family and long reigns. It does not receive invented donations, judgements or scientific experiments.
